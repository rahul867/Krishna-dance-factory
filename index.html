About the studio

A room where
nobody is watching

Krishna Dance Factory started in a single hall in Gurugram with one rule: you don't need rhythm to begin, you need to begin to find rhythm. Nine years later that rule hasn't moved.

  • Beginner-first batches — no partner or experience required.
  • Max 12 per class — everyone gets corrected, every session.
  • Socials & showcases — practise what you learn on a real floor.
