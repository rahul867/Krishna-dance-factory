About the studio

A floor where
everyone starts from zero

Krishna Dance Factory was founded in Gurugram with a simple rule: you don't need rhythm to start, you just need a welcoming room and the right teacher. Fourteen years later, that promise remains unchanged.

Whether you are a working professional de-stressing after a busy week at Horizon Centre or CyberHub, a couple looking for a fun weekend date night, or learning solo — every step is broken down with personal attention and zero pressure.

  • 100% Founder-Taught — Every minute taught directly by Krishna (14+ years mastery). Zero junior assistant hand-offs.
  • Intimate Batch Size (Max 12) — Real personal correction, so you actually learn without getting lost in a crowd.
  • No Partner Required — Full rotation in class. Solo beginners and couples are equally welcome.
  • Doorstep Convenience — Studio sessions in DLF Phase 5 or private at-home lessons across all of Gurugram.
