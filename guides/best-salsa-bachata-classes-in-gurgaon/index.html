5 Things to Check Before Choosing a Salsa Studio in Gurgaon

1. Batch Size & Attention:

If there are 25+ people in the room, the teacher cannot see if your weight transfer or timing is off. Look for studios that cap batches at 10–12 dancers.

2. Who Actually Teaches?

Ensure the person listed on the website is the one teaching your class, rather than an untrained junior assistant.

3. Partner Rotation Policy:

Top social studios rotate partners during class. This ensures you learn true lead-follow dynamics and never feel left out if you come solo.

4. Location & Parking:

Look for studio locations with easy parking and metro access (e.g. DLF Phase 5 / Golf Course Road) so attending weekend sessions is frictionless.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do I need to bring a partner to join?

No. Most Gurgaon Latin studios rotate partners in every session. You can join completely solo.

What is the typical fee for Salsa classes in Gurgaon?

Fees typically range from ₹2,500 to ₹4,000 per month for weekend group batches.

What should I wear for my first class?

Wear comfortable casual clothes and flat shoes or sneakers that allow easy turning on wooden floors. Special dance heels or Latin shoes are not needed for beginners.
