Private bachata lessons

Get comfortable
moving together.

Start with the basic rhythm and a comfortable frame. Learn how to lead or follow a movement without pulling, guessing or rushing the next step.

As those foundations settle, we can spend more time on footwork, body movement and responding to the music. You can ask for another explanation whenever you need one.

Before your first class

A few things
you might be wondering.

If your question isn’t here, send Krishna a message. It’s fine to ask before you book.

I’ve never danced before. Can I start here?

Yes. You can begin with finding the beat and shifting your weight, then add steps as you get comfortable. Tell Krishna you’re a complete beginner so he can start at the right point for you.

Do I need to bring a partner?

You can come solo. Krishna can lead or follow during practice. You’re also welcome to book as a couple and learn together.

When can I book a private lesson?

Private lessons are arranged directly with Krishna. Send your preferred days and times, and he’ll confirm which studio or at-home slots are available.

How many lessons will I need?

That depends on your starting point, what you want to learn and how much you practice. Share your goal or event date with Krishna so you can discuss a session plan. You don’t have to decide on a long course before that conversation.

What do private classes cost?

Fees depend on solo or couple lessons, the session plan and whether you learn at the studio or at home. Ask Krishna for the options and confirm the price before booking.

Can the class happen at my home?

At-home lessons are available across Gurgaon by arrangement. Share your location and preferred time with Krishna. You’ll need roughly 3 × 3 metres of clear floor space to practice comfortably.
