Pillar 01 · Rhythm & Expression

Bollywood dance
& storytelling.

Children don’t need boring technical counts—they need music that makes their feet move automatically. We take the energetic tracks they already hum at home and turn that restless energy into rhythm, expressive storytelling, and big, genuine smiles.

What your child will master

  • Rhythm & beat-matching: Clapping, stepping, and counting beats naturally without getting confused.

  • Stage presence: Natural poise and confidence for school annual days and family celebrations.

Ask Krishna a question ↗
What age groups do you teach?

We teach children aged 4 to 14 years old. Batches are grouped by age (4–7 years and 8–14 years) so your child learns alongside peers at their physical and coordination level.

How do I enroll my child in the weekend batch?

Simply message or call Krishna on WhatsApp (+91 95993 53649) with your child's age. We will share the current batch schedule, curriculum details, and guide you through registration.

Does my child need previous dance experience?

Not at all! Most children join us as complete beginners. The curriculum starts with basic rhythm and simple steps, building up gradually so no child feels overwhelmed.

When are the kids dance classes held?

Classes run on weekends (Saturday and Sunday). Timings are planned around school and homework schedules. Contact Krishna for the latest slot availability for your child's age group.

Can parents sit and watch the class?

Yes, absolutely. Our studio features comfortable parent seating. You are welcome to observe the session and see how happily your child is engaging.

What should my child wear to class?

Comfortable clothing like a t-shirt, track pants or leggings, and clean sports shoes or sneakers. We also recommend bringing a water bottle.

What are the class fees?

Fees depend on the chosen batch and duration. Message or call Krishna on +91 95993 53649 for the current fee breakdown and available batch timings.

Where is the studio located?

Krishna Dance Factory is at D4/4, Club Drive, DLF Exclusive Floors, Sector 53, DLF Phase 5, Gurugram, Haryana 122009 (near DLF Club5 and Golf Course Road).
