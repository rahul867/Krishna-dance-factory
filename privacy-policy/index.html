Your information & your choices

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Krishna Dance Factory (KDF), D4/4, Club Drive, DLF Exclusive Floors, Sector 53, DLF Phase 5, Gurugram, Haryana 122009, handles information when you visit our website or contact us about classes.

Information we handle

When you call, email or message us, you may provide your name, contact details, class interests, preferred timings and enquiry or booking information. Please share only what is needed to answer your enquiry. For children’s classes, a parent or guardian should contact us and manage the enquiry; please avoid sending unnecessary details about a child.

With your analytics permission, Google Analytics 4 measures visits, page views, referral/campaign information, browser/device information, approximate location and interactions such as call, WhatsApp and directions clicks. A contact-button click is an expression of interest, not confirmation of a conversation or booking.

Why we use information

We use enquiry information to reply, explain classes and fees, arrange bookings and provide studio services. Optional website analytics helps us improve usability and content. Advertising measurement helps us understand which advertisements lead to visits and contact actions. Where you allow advertising features, data may also be used to build audiences and personalise advertising.

Google Analytics and Google Ads

We use Google Analytics and link it with Google Ads for reporting and conversion measurement. Our ads use campaign parameters and may use Google click identifiers to attribute activity. Our Google Ads link has advertising personalisation enabled; website consent choices control the signals sent by our website tags. Analytics and advertising providers may use cookies or similar identifiers according to your choices.

Google and other advertising vendors may show advertisements on other websites based on earlier visits where personalised advertising is enabled and permitted. You can manage Google advertising choices through My Ad Center. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Calls from advertisements

We use Google Ads call reporting. Calls from advertisements may be routed through a Google forwarding number to our studio number. Google processes call information such as call time, duration and connection status for advertising reporting and conversion measurement. Call reporting is different from audio recording; this website setup does not enable audio recording of your phone conversations.

Microsoft Clarity: heatmaps and session replay

With analytics permission, Microsoft Clarity captures website interaction information, including clicks, scrolling, behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replays, to help us understand and improve our website. Session replay refers to website interaction, not telephone audio. Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies, and receives advertising consent signals separately. Microsoft processes information under its Privacy Statement.

Meta Pixel

With advertising permission, Meta Pixel sends website PageView events and associated technical information to Meta, which may include page URLs, identifiers, browser/device information and network information. Meta may match activity with its users to measure advertisements and support advertising audiences and personalisation. Our website installation sends PageView; it does not treat a page visit as an admission or send WhatsApp conversation contents to Meta. Read Meta’s Privacy Policy and manage your Meta ad preferences.

Cookies, storage and your controls

Optional analytics and advertising scripts remain blocked until you grant the corresponding permission. Select Accept all, Reject optional or Manage choices in the banner. You can reopen in any page footer to change or withdraw permission. Withdrawing previously granted permission reloads the page to stop already loaded optional scripts. This does not undo data already processed.

We store your consent choices and their timestamp in first-party local storage (kdf-consent-v1) for up to 180 days, then ask again. If your browser blocks storage, your choices may need to be selected again on another page. Session storage may preserve an enquiry’s referral/campaign source while you navigate and can prefill a source label in a WhatsApp enquiry; you can edit the message before sending it.

When enabled, providers may set identifiers such as Google Analytics _ga cookies, Clarity _clck/_clsk cookies and Meta _fbp/_fbc cookies. Names and lifetimes can vary with provider settings and browser behavior. You can clear site storage through your browser. Our controls attempt to clear accessible tracking cookies when consent is withdrawn; cookies on third-party domains must be managed through those providers or your browser.

Maps and external contact services

Our class pages embed Google Maps. Loading an embedded map connects your browser to Google and may expose technical information such as your IP address; Google governs its own processing. WhatsApp, Instagram, email and telephone links open the corresponding external service when selected. If you send an enquiry, that service processes the information you choose to send under its own terms. A link to Instagram does not by itself install a Meta Pixel.

Sharing, storage and retention

Information may be processed by the studio, the analytics/advertising and communication providers described above, hosting/security providers and service providers necessary to operate the website or respond to enquiries. Providers may process information outside India. We may disclose information when required by law.

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as reasonably needed to handle enquiries, provide services, resolve issues and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Analytics, advertising and session-replay retention also depends on provider settings and their policies. Consent-choice storage lasts up to 180 days; referral session storage normally lasts for the browser tab’s session. Contact us to ask about a particular record or request deletion.

Your questions and requests

To ask about your information, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email krishnachoreographer@gmail.com or call +91 95993 53649. Include enough information for us to identify your enquiry without sending unnecessary sensitive information. We may need to verify your identity and retain information required for legitimate service or legal purposes.

Policy updates

We will update this page when our practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
