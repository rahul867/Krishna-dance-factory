Beginner Salsa & Bachata classes

Two different vibes.
One great feeling.

Latin dancing isn’t about being flawless—it’s about knowing what to do when that moment comes. Here is how Salsa and Bachata feel on the dance floor, and what you’ll master in class.

Beginner Salsa classes

Salsa classes
for beginners.

“Normal” salsa doesn’t exist. Forget robotic 1-2-3 counts or memorizing rigid routines. Salsa is about communicating without speaking—spinning, laughing off a wrong step, and keeping the flow. You’ll learn how to hear the rhythm, lead or follow naturally, and feel confident at any Latin social night.

What you’ll gain in class

  • Rhythm & timing: hearing the beat so you never second-guess your feet.
  • Smooth turns & footwork: moving fluidly without feeling stiff or awkward.
  • Effortless connection: how to lead or follow any partner in a social setting.

Beginner Bachata classes

Bachata classes
for beginners.

A great Bachata track comes on. You recognize the guitar melody. You want to dance, but you stay back hoping no one asks you. Bachata is pure groove and connection—slower, smoother, and deeply musical. We strip away the overthinking so your body just flows with the beat.

What you’ll gain in class

  • The signature 4-count hip groove and smooth footwork.
  • Natural body wave and posture without weird posing.
  • Partner musicality: moving together so everything just makes sense.

Ask Krishna a question ↗
Do I need to bring a partner?

Not at all. Over 80% of our students join solo. We rotate partners frequently during drills so everyone gets to dance with everyone. (Coming as a couple? You’re completely welcome to stay paired up throughout the class!).

I’ve never danced before. Can I join?

Yes! This batch is designed precisely for people who have two left feet or have never set foot on a dance floor. We don’t assume any prior coordination or rhythm—we build it from scratch, step by step.

When are the classes held?

Classes run in weekend batches on Saturday and Sunday. Contact Krishna for the latest time slots and available batches.

How do I join a Salsa or Bachata batch?

WhatsApp or call Krishna for the current batch timings, course fees and joining details. He can help you choose a batch for your starting level.

What are the course fees?

Fees depend on the batch and programme. Message Krishna for the current fee structure and available timings before booking.
