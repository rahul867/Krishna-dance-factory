Beginner Salsa & Bachata classes

What you’ll learn in
Salsa & Bachata.

Salsa and Bachata are Latin partner dances. In both classes, you’ll learn basic steps, timing, turns and how to dance with a partner. Here’s what you’ll practise in each style.

Beginner Salsa classes

Salsa classes
for beginners.

Learn basic Salsa steps, timing, footwork, turns and partner work. Practise simple combinations with different partners to prepare for social dancing.

What you’ll practise

  • Basic Salsa steps and how to stay on the beat.
  • Simple turns and footwork combinations.
  • How to lead or follow a partner’s steps.

Beginner Bachata classes

Bachata classes
for beginners.

Start with basic Bachata steps and learn to move in time with the music. Practise body movement, simple turns and moving together with a partner for social dancing.

What you’ll practise

  • Basic Bachata steps and timing.
  • Simple turns and controlled body movement.
  • How to move in time with a partner.

Ask Krishna a question ↗
Do I need to bring a partner?

No. Come solo. Partners rotate during class, so you can practise with different dancers.

I’ve never danced before. Can I join?

Yes. Beginner batches start with timing, basic steps and fundamentals. You don’t need any previous dance experience.

When are the classes held?

Classes run in weekend batches on Saturday and Sunday. Contact Krishna for the latest time slots and available batches.

How do I join a Salsa or Bachata batch?

WhatsApp or call Krishna for the current batch timings, course fees and joining details. He can help you choose a batch for your starting level.

What are the course fees?

Fees depend on the batch and programme. Message Krishna for the current fee structure and available timings before booking.

Where are the Salsa and Bachata classes held?

Krishna Dance Factory is at D4/4, Club Drive, DLF Exclusive Floors, Sector 53, DLF Phase 5, Gurugram, Haryana 122009, near Golf Course Road.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable clothes that let you move freely and clean, comfortable footwear suitable for a dance floor.
